# Product: SentinelChain

## Identity & Positioning
- **Product Name**: SentinelChain
- **Positioning**: Autonomous Supply Chain Intelligence. From Global Disruption to Procurement Action.
- **Core Category**: AI-Powered Supply Chain Resilience & Autonomous Procurement Platform for Electronics Manufacturing.
- **Platform**: Web (Next.js 16 + React)

## Target Audience
- Supply-chain executives (CSCOs, VPs of Global Operations).
- Procurement & sourcing managers.
- High-mix & mission-critical electronics manufacturers (Automotive, Industrial, Aerospace, Medical).
- Enterprise risk management teams.

## Core Capabilities & Workflows
1. **Global Disruption Intelligence**: Continuous multi-source regulatory, seismic, and disclosure telemetry (USGS Seismic M4+, Federal Register BIS Export Administration Regulations, SEC 10-Q/8-K, Foundry Investor Disclosures).
2. **AI Threat Validation**: Noise filtering, tier-weighted signal confidence evaluation, and entity extraction.
3. **Private SAP S/4HANA Correlation**: Secure correlation of external events against private enterprise Bills of Materials (BOM) via `API_BILL_OF_MATERIAL_SRV` to isolate impacted component MPNs, assembly lines, and plant inventories.
4. **Financial Exposure Analysis**: Real-time calculation of daily revenue at risk based on production velocity and inventory days-of-supply (DoS).
5. **Alternative Component Discovery**: Live API integration with franchised distributor inventories (Mouser Electronics) verifying form-fit-function pin-compatibility, in-stock quantity, factory lead time, and spot pricing.
6. **Autonomous Procurement via SAP Ariba**: Automated generation of compliant Purchase Requisitions (PR) executed with a hard +10% price ceiling safety guardrail.
7. **Resilience Planning & Ledger Audit**: Immutable execution trace of all automated triage and sourcing decisions.

## Verified Integrations
- **SAP S/4HANA**: Private ERP connectivity, BOM explosion, material inventory, and assembly line mapping.
- **SAP Ariba**: Purchase Requisition creation and procurement guardrail execution.
- **Mouser Electronics**: Real-time component discovery, live stock verification, and lead-time comparison.
- **Institutional Telemetry Sources**: USGS, Federal Register (BIS EAR), SEC EDGAR, Foundry Disclosures.

## Visual & Brand Constraints
- **Color Palette**: Pure black (`#050608`) and obsidian charcoal backgrounds; metallic silver typography and accents (`#E2E8F0`, `#94A3B8`); crisp white text; subtle silver metallic gradients; restrained glass effects; fine technical hairline grids.
- **Strict Anti-Patterns**: No generic purple AI gradients; no playful consumer SaaS illustrations; no random floating AI sparkle icons; no repetitive bubbly rounded cards; no orange startup-style themes on the landing page.
- **Brand Assets**: Authentic SentinelChain metallic silver hexagonal "S" emblem with global network, transport truck, orbital ring, and "SentinelChain" wordmark (`/logo.png`, `/shield.png`, `/logo-dark.png`).
