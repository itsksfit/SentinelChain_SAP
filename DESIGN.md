# Design System: SentinelChain Enterprise Intelligence

A pure black, metallic silver, enterprise-grade intelligence platform for mission-critical electronics manufacturing and autonomous supply chain remediation.

## Overview
- **Visual Thesis**: Wall Street / Aerospace Mission Control Rigidity. Pure black, deep obsidian charcoal, polished metallic silver accents, white typography, and fine technical grid structures.
- **Strict Anti-Patterns**: No generic purple AI gradients; no playful consumer SaaS illustrations; no random floating AI sparkle icons; no repetitive bubbly rounded cards; no orange startup-style themes on the landing page.
- **Brand Authority**: Authentic SentinelChain metallic silver hexagonal "S" emblem with global network, transport truck, orbital ring, and "SentinelChain" wordmark (`/logo.png`, `/shield.png`).

## Colors
- **Canvas**: Pure Black (`#050608`).
- **Surfaces**: Deep Charcoal (`#0A0D12`), Elevated Container (`#10141D`), Card Surface (`#161B26`).
- **Borders**: 1px Hairlines (`border-white/[0.08]` to `border-white/20`).
- **Metallic Silver Typography**: Subtle linear metallic gradients (`from-white via-[#E2E8F0] to-[#94A3B8]`).
- **Status Indicators**:
  - `status-critical` (`#EF4444`): Single-source disruption, plant stoppage risk.
  - `status-warning` (`#F59E0B`): Buffer threshold alert, allocation strain.
  - `status-success` (`#10B981`): Sourced replacement, ERP sync verified.
  - `status-telemetry` (`#06B6D4`): Real-time sensor / API stream active.

## Typography
- **Primary Typeface**: Inter, sans-serif. High legibility, crisp rendering on dark OLED and Retina displays.
- **Monospace Typeface**: SF Mono, Menlo, monospace for SAP material codes, MPNs, coordinates, hashes, and latencies.
- **Hierarchy**: Minimum text size is 11px (`text-[11px]`). Clear structural step-downs between Display, Headline, Title, Body, and Telemetry Labels.

## Components
- **Metallic Buttons**: High-density pill and rounded-xl containers with 1px silver border, subtle radial highlight, and tactile hover states.
- **Technical Pipeline Spine**: Connected data bus with sequential stage nodes, pulse indicators, and live data telemetry.
- **Integration Nodes**: Clean architectural badges for SAP S/4HANA, SAP Ariba, Mouser, and regulatory feeds with live status pings.
